Humphrey Visual Field 24-2 Test

This test evaluates your peripheral vision using the standard 24-2 grid pattern with 54 test points.

Instructions:
  • Keep your eyes focused on the red cross (+) in the center at all times
  • Tap the screen when you see a white dot appear in your peripheral vision
  • You have 500ms to respond when a stimulus appears
  • Do NOT move your eyes away from the center cross
  • Tap anywhere on screen when you see a stimulus
Test Features:
  • 4-2 Staircase methodology for threshold detection
  • Goldmann Size III stimuli (0.43° diameter)
  • Automatic reliability testing (False Positives/Negatives)
  • Real-time progress tracking
Mobile Instructions:

For best results on mobile devices:

  • Enable fullscreen mode when prompted
  • Use landscape orientation if possible
  • Silence notifications during test
